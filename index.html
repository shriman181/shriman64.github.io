The problem

A $14 billion industry running on guesswork

Every day, pawnbrokers across America appraise thousands of items — watches, jewellery, electronics, instruments — using personal experience, printed blue books, and instinct. The result is inconsistent valuations, mispriced loan portfolios, and a blind spot for stolen goods.

Serova replaces the guesswork with a verifiable, real-time intelligence layer. Snap a photo. Get a certified appraisal, a fraud check, and a loan recommendation — in seconds.

  • 01
    No standardised valuation
    Appraisals vary by 30–60% across shops for identical items. Staff turnover compounds the inconsistency daily.
  • 02
    Stolen goods liability
    Pawnshops are required to cross-check items against police databases — yet most do it manually, slowly, or not at all.
  • 03
    Dead loan capital
    Over-valued collateral erodes margins. Under-valued items drives customers to competitors. No operator has portfolio-level visibility.
  • 04
    Zero intelligence software
    Existing POS systems log transactions but offer no predictive analytics, no market pricing, and no AI — they are digital ledgers.
The platform

Intelligence built for the
counter

Three interlocking modules that work together — or drop into your existing POS as an API.

01

Lens — Image Valuation

Photograph any item. Serova's computer vision identifies make, model, condition grade, and authenticity markers across jewellery, watches, electronics, and instruments. Returns a certified appraisal with confidence score in under 8 seconds.

02

Shield — Fraud & Theft Detection

Every item is automatically cross-referenced against NCIC, local police databases, and Serova's proprietary stolen goods registry. Serial numbers are extracted from images. Compliance reports are generated automatically for regulatory submissions.

Aggregate market pricing across eBay, auction houses, and dealer networks to dynamically optimise your loan-to-value ratios. Identify which items in your portfolio are appreciating, depreciating, or at risk — before the forfeit date arrives.

How it works

From photo to
appraisal in seconds

The entire workflow happens at the counter. No specialist knowledge required.

01

Photograph the item

Staff take 2–3 photos on any device. Serova's vision model reads condition, brand markings, model identifiers, and any visible serial numbers.

02

AI classification & authentication

The item is classified against 40M+ product records. Counterfeit signals are flagged in real-time using hallmark analysis and known fake databases.

03

Fraud & database check

Serial numbers and descriptions are checked against NCIC and partner police databases simultaneously. Result in under 3 seconds.

04

Receive certified appraisal

A loan recommendation, retail estimate, and condition report are returned — ready to print, display to the customer, or push to your POS system.

The founder
"Every pawnbroker I spoke to had the same look — they knew they were leaving money on the table, they just didn't know how much. Serova makes that visible."

Saksham Jain founded Serova after spending months embedded in pawnshops across the US, watching brokers appraise items with printed blue books and twenty-year-old industry knowledge. He saw a $14 billion industry with almost no software intelligence — and a gap that computer vision and real-time market data could close overnight.

His focus is on building AI that earns trust at the counter — not in the boardroom. Serova's models are trained on actual pawn transaction data, with valuations validated by certified appraisers to ensure the system works for operators, not against them.
